# Clawdular Genome Studio - Promo Video Prompt

## Video Overview

**Title**: CLAWDULAR GENOME STUDIO - The Evolution of AI Skills  
**Duration**: 60-90 seconds  
**Style**: Cinematic tech documentary meets retro-futuristic NASA aesthetic  
**Tone**: Mysterious, powerful, revolutionary  
**Music**: Cinematic ambient-electronic score with biological tension, building to climax

## Post-Production Effects

### Required Effects
1. **CRT Shader**
   - Scan lines (horizontal)
   - Screen curvature (subtle)
   - Phosphor glow
   - Vignette
   - Slight flicker

2. **Chromatic Aberration**
   - At screen edges
   - On fast motion
   - Very subtle

3. **Film Grain**
   - 35mm film grain overlay
   - 10-15% opacity
   - Adds texture

4. **Bloom/Glow**
   - On bright elements
   - White elements only
   - Subtle, not overwhelming

5. **Motion Blur**
   - On camera moves
   - On fast-moving data packets

### Color Grading
- Desaturate to 95% (near monochrome)
- Lift blacks slightly
- Crush whites slightly
- Add subtle blue tint to shadows (optional)
- Keep highlights warm white
